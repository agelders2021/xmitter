# xmitter Rev D: operational description

Date: 2026-11-13

Basis: netlists exported 2026-11-12 (Output LPF) and 2026-11-13 (ss_pa, analog,
second analog export), plus R83, which was added after the last export and is
recorded here from the designer's description.

## 1. Overview

xmitter is a 10 W class CW transmitter for the 40, 20, 17 and 15 m bands. It is
built on three boards (analog, SS PA, output LPF) around an Adafruit Metro
ESP32-S3 and runs from a 13.8 V shack supply.

RF is generated by a Si5351A, keyed and shaped in a dual-gate MOSFET, buffered,
amplified by a push-pull BS170 driver and a push-pull IRF510 final, and filtered
by a relay-selected seven-element low-pass filter.

A hardware fault latch removes PA bias, driver bias and RF drive within about
1 ms of an over-current. A heartbeat watchdog removes PA and relay power if
firmware stops.

A local sidetone chain on the analog board uses the spare fourth switch in the
fault-shutdown CD74HC4066 (U202) to gate a Metro-generated audio square wave
with GATE_2, so the audible envelope is time-locked to the RF envelope, and
an LM386 drives a small on-board speaker.

## 2. Signal chain

### 2.1 Analog board: clock to J1

1. **Clock.** U5 (Si5351A breakout) CLK2 passes RS2 (0 Ω) to R18 (50 Ω to GND),
   then C26 (100 nF) to gate 1 of Q2 (40673). CLK0 and CLK1 are unused.
2. **Gate 1 bias.** R14 (39 kΩ) and R19 (10 kΩ) from +12 V give 2.45 V,
   bypassed by C10 and fed to the gate through R21 (100 kΩ). C26 with R21 gives
   a 10 ms time constant.
3. **Keying and envelope.** Gate 2 is driven by U11 (TL071) through R20 (100 Ω)
   with C13 (100 pF) to GND. U11 scales and offsets MCP4922 channel B (equation
   in 4.7). A DAC output of 0 V gives about -4.3 V on gate 2 (muted); full scale
   gives about +4.9 V. There is no separate key line; firmware keys by writing
   the envelope DAC.
4. **Q2 stage.** Source: R9 (220 Ω) bypassed by C5. Drain: R31 (150 Ω) to
   +12 V. At about 15 mA the drain sits near 9.8 V.
5. **Drain low-pass.** C9 (10 nF), R1 (50 Ω), L1 (270 nH), then C1 (120 pF) and
   R11 (10 kΩ) to GND at U1's input. With R31 as the source resistance the
   corner is about 6.4 MHz.

   | Band | Transimpedance, drain current to U1 input |
   |---|---|
   | 40 m | 102 Ω |
   | 20 m | 64 Ω |
   | 17 m | 52 Ω |
   | 15 m | 45 Ω |

   The 7 dB slope is compensated in firmware per band. The clock's third
   harmonic is reduced by 7 to 10 dB relative to the fundamental.
6. **Buffer.** U1 (LM7171) runs at a gain of 2 with R8 = R5 = 3 kΩ and C23
   (2 pF) across R8. The feedback capacitor trims the gain to about 1.7 at
   21 MHz. Output goes through R2 (50 Ω) and C4 (100 nF) to SMA J1. Into the
   50 Ω PA input, half of U1's output voltage appears at J1.

### 2.2 SS PA board: J3 to J1

1. **Input transformer T201** (BN61-2402). Primary from J3 to GND. The
   centre-tapped secondary drives the BS170 gates directly (pin 3 to Q6, pin 5
   to Q2). R17 and R18 (220 Ω) from each gate to the centre tap terminate the
   input at about 49 Ω for a 2 : 3+3 winding. The centre tap carries
   DRIVER_BIAS through R21 (100 Ω), bypassed by C21 and C24.
2. **Driver.** Q2 and Q6 (BS170) with 2.2 Ω source resistors (R15, R16). Drains
   to T202's primary ends; its centre tap is fed from the 13.8 V PA rail
   through L18, bypassed by C17 and C18.
3. **Interstage T202** (BN61-2402). The secondary is loaded by R13 and R14
   (220 Ω from each end to GND) and coupled through C4 and C5 to the IRF510
   gates.
4. **Finals.** Q3 and Q4 (IRF510). Each has drain-to-gate feedback of 0.1 µF
   100 V, 220 Ω 2 W and 1 µH in series (C8/R6/L2 on Q3, C7/R5/L1 on Q4). Gate
   bias arrives through L8 or L9, a 47 Ω resistor (R8 or R7), a 0.1 µF bypass
   (C10 or C9) and a 1 µH choke (L4 or L3). PA_Bias_A serves Q3; PA_Bias_B
   serves Q4.
5. **Source sensing.** Each source returns through two 0.22 Ω 2 W resistors in
   parallel (0.11 Ω), bypassed by two 220 nF chip capacitors and a 100 nF disc.
   U1 (MCP6002, on +5 V from the umbilical) amplifies each source voltage by 10
   and sends it to the analog board.
6. **Drain feed and output.** T203 (FT50-43 bifilar) feeds 13.8 V to both
   drains. C3 (33 pF 200 V) sits across the drains. T204 (BN43-202, 2:3 turns)
   presents 22 Ω drain to drain from a 50 Ω load and drives SMA J1.
7. **Supply entry.** J2 screw terminal, L5 choke with D2 across it as a clamp,
   then C6 (4700 µF), C1 and C2.

At 10 W each drain swings 10.5 V peak with 1.9 A peaks. Supply current is about
1.2 A, class-B efficiency about 61 %, and each IRF510 dissipates about 3 W.

### 2.3 Output LPF board: J28 to antenna

- **Filters.** Four seven-element low-pass filters (shunt C, series L) with
  200 V C0G capacitors and T50-6 inductors. Both poles of a band relay switch
  together, and each idle filter is terminated in 50 Ω at both ends.

  | Band | Relay | Shunt C (pF) | Inductors (target nH, turns) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 40 m | K4 | 470, 820, 820, 470 | 1416 (19 T), 1565 (20 T), 1416 (19 T) |
  | 20 m | K3 | 220, 390, 390, 220 | 662 (13 T), 733 (14 T), 662 (13 T) |
  | 17 m | K2 | 180, 330, 330, 180 | 542 (12 T), 599 (12 T), 542 (12 T) |
  | 15 m | K1 | 150, 270, 270, 150 | 452 (11 T), 499 (11 T), 452 (11 T) |

  On 17 m and 15 m the three coils have equal turn counts; the centre coil is
  brought about 10 % above the outer pair by turn spacing (see section 8).
- **T/R relay K5.** Operated: filter output to antenna jack J1. Released:
  antenna to receiver jack J3 through clamp diodes D1/D2 and R19. Receive
  bypasses the filters.
- **Status.** K5's second pole grounds TRANSMIT_N (J2 pin 7) when operated.
  There is no receive status line.
- **Drivers.** One 2N7000 per relay, driven from the analog board's port
  expander through RC filters, with a 1N4148 across each coil. Coil supply is
  SS_PWR_PRE through R28 (62 Ω).

### 2.4 Analog board: sidetone chain

The sidetone shares the RF chain's keying signal (GATE_2) so the audible envelope
matches the RF envelope without any additional firmware state. The tone itself is
generated by Metro A1 (GPIO15) as an ESP32-S3 LEDC PWM output: firmware programs
the LEDC channel for a 500 to 1000 Hz square wave at 50 % duty (default 700 Hz)
and then leaves it free-running. Keying is done downstream by the fourth switch
of U202 (CD74HC4066), not by starting and stopping the LEDC. There is no active
device in the audio path between the Metro pin and the LM386, so there is no
standing current and no keying pedestal.

1. **Audio switch.** U202 switch 4: pin 11 (4Y) is the Sidetone net from Metro
   A1, pin 10 (4Z) is Sidetone_Out. The switch's roughly 100 Ω on-resistance
   is negligible into the DC-block capacitor that follows.
2. **Enable from GATE_2.** U202 pin 12 (4E) is driven by GATE_2 through R93
   (10 kΩ) and clamped to GND by D8 (1N5711 Schottky, anode to GND, cathode
   to 4E). GATE_2 swings between about -4.3 V (muted) and +4.9 V (keyed); the
   4066 enable absolute-max is -0.5 V. On the negative swing R93 limits current
   into D8 to about 0.4 mA and D8 holds 4E near -0.25 V, inside the 4066's
   rating. On the positive swing D8 is reverse-biased and 4E follows GATE_2
   to about +4.9 V through R93, well above the 4066's logic-high threshold.
   R93 and D8 live on the fault_shutdown sheet with the rest of the U202
   gating network.
3. **DC block and volume.** C70 (100 nF 50 V) removes the roughly +1.65 V DC
   content of the switched 0 to 3.3 V square wave. RV2 (10 kΩ 25-turn Bourns
   trimmer) sets the sidetone level: pin 1 is the top, the wiper feeds the
   LM386 non-inverting input, pin 3 is GND. With PWM amplitude of 3.3 V peak
   to peak at the pot top and an LM386 gain of 20, the trimmer is normally
   set well below mid-rotation to keep the LM386 input below its roughly
   0.5 V peak-to-peak clipping threshold.
4. **Amplifier.** U19 (LM386) runs at its default gain of 20 with pins 1, 7
   and 8 unconnected. Pin 2 (-IN) is tied to GND. The +12 V rail is bypassed
   by C75 (100 µF 35 V) at pin 6. Output Zobel is C73 (47 nF 50 V) and R92
   (10 Ω) in series from pin 5 to GND.
5. **Output.** C74 (220 µF 35 V) couples U19 pin 5 to J4 pin 1, a 2-pin
   screw terminal for an 8 Ω speaker. J4 pin 2 is GND. With 8 Ω the output
   high-pass corner is near 90 Hz.

### 2.5 Operating classes

| | Class AB | Class C |
|---|---|---|
| PA bias (MCP4728 A, B) | about 3.8 V, set per device | 1 to 2 V |
| Drive | lower envelope setting | higher envelope setting, or lower R5 for more buffer gain |
| Expected output | about 5 W | about 10 W |
| Keying shape | follows the envelope DAC | distorted by the PA threshold; pre-distort the ramp |

The hardware is the same for both.

## 3. Control architecture

The Metro ESP32-S3 (U16) sits on the analog board. It is powered from +12 V
through polyfuse F1 and J21; its own 5 V and 3.3 V pins are not used.

A third shifter channel carries the MCP4728 LDAC line from Metro A3.

| PCF8575 | U17 | 0x20 | Control port (3.2) |

### 3.3 SPI and the MCP4922

MOSI (D11), SCK (D13), chip select (D10) and LDAC (A4) pass through U9
(74HCT125) to U4. R23, R24, R25 and R37 hold the lines in a defined state
during boot.

- **Channel A (U4 pin 14)** is DRIVER_BIAS, sent to the PA board on J31 pin 6.
- **Channel B (U4 pin 10)** is the envelope, sent to U11 through R13.
- **Reference.** Both reference pins (11 and 13) are fed from FAULT_N through
  R47 (1 kΩ), with C48 (22 nF) to GND. Full scale is therefore the FAULT_N high
  level, about 4.96 V, and both outputs collapse when a fault is latched. The
  buffered-reference setting is used so the DAC does not load FAULT_N.

### 3.5 Front panel

The 20 x 4 LCD is driven in 8-bit mode from U10 (data on P0 to P7, E on P11, RS
on P12, R/W grounded). Backlight colours are switched by Q1, Q3 and Q4 from P8
to P10. The MBL-600 tuning encoder connects on ENC5 with 120 Ω terminations
(R57, R58).

## 4. Fault shutdown

A hardware latch on the analog board trips when the average source current of
either IRF510 exceeds a trimmer-set limit between about 0.1 and 2.9 A. It does
not depend on firmware except to be reset.

### 4.1 How it works

1. **Sense.** Each pre-amplified source signal arrives on J31 (pins 1 and 4),
   is halved by R65/R68 or R66/R67, and filtered by L208/C223/C229 or
   L209/C224/C228. This node also feeds the Metro ADC.
2. **Compare.** U201 (LM393) compares each filtered signal, through R221 or
   R222 with a 4.7 MΩ pull-up to +5 V, against V_REF. Either output going low
   pulls FAULT_N_RAW low.
3. **Latch.** U202 switch 3 connects FAULT_N_RAW to FAULT_N. R16 feeds FAULT_N
   back into V_REF, so when FAULT_N falls V_REF drops below anything the sense
   inputs can reach and the comparator holds itself tripped. D9 limits how far
   below ground V_REF goes.
4. **Act.** FAULT_N low opens U202 switches 1 and 2 (PA_Bias_A and PA_Bias_B
   are disconnected from the MCP4728 and discharged by R219 and R212),
   collapses the MCP4922 reference (DRIVER_BIAS and the envelope go to near
   zero), and reads low at Metro A2.
5. **Reset.** A high-to-low edge on FAULT_CLEAR fires U18B for 1 ms
   (R61 x C19). Its inverted output opens switch 3, R46 pulls FAULT_N high,
   V_REF recovers, and the comparator releases if the current is below the
   limit. If the fault persists, the latch re-trips when switch 3 closes.

### 4.2 Symbols

| Symbol | Meaning | Nominal |
|---|---|---|
| I | Average source current of one IRF510 | |
| R_S | Source sense resistance (0.22 Ω ∥ 0.22 Ω) | 0.11 Ω |
| G | PA board pre-amp gain (1 + 9 k / 1 k) | 10 |
| S | Voltage at the Source_Sense node on the analog board | |
| R_D | Source resistance of the R65/R68 divider | 5 kΩ |
| V5 | +5 V rail | 5.0 V |
| F | FAULT_N voltage | 4.96 V high; 0.3 to 0.75 V low |
| V_N | Negative rail at R59 | -11.6 V under load |
| a | RV1 position, 0 = wiper at pin 1, 1 = wiper at pin 3 | 0 to 1 |
| V_m | Voltage at the LM393 "-" input | |

### 4.3 Sense chain

    S = I * R_S * G * R68 / (R65 + R68)
      = I * 0.11 * 10 * 0.5
      = 0.55 * I                              [V, I in A]

    V_m = S * R224 / (R224 + R221 + R_D) + V5 * (R221 + R_D) / (R224 + R221 + R_D)
        = 0.979 * S + 0.109
        = 0.538 * I + 0.109                   [V]

The 0.109 V pedestal keeps the "-" input above ground at zero current. The ADC
tap sits ahead of R221 and sees only about 5 mV of it, so firmware uses
I = V / 0.55.

### 4.4 Reference network

Values: R16 = 100 kΩ, R17 = 75 kΩ, R41 = 3.3 kΩ, R59 = 27 kΩ, RV1 = 10 kΩ.

With r1 = a * RV1 (pin 1 to wiper) and r2 = (1 - a) * RV1 (wiper to pin 3),
looking into RV1 pin 1:

    V_T = V_N * (r2 + R41) / (R59 + r2 + R41)
    R_T = r1 + R59 ∥ (r2 + R41)

    V_REF = F + (V_T - F) * R16 / (R16 + R17 + R_T)

### 4.5 Trip current

    I_trip = (V_REF - 0.109) / 0.538          [A]

With F = 4.96 V and V_N = -11.6 V:

| RV1 position | V_REF | Trip per FET | Latched V_REF before the clamp (F = 0.3 to 0.75 V) | FAULT_N needed to release |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 % | 0.18 V | 0.13 A | -1.94 to -1.74 V | 4.91 V |
| 25 % | 0.49 V | 0.71 A | -1.65 to -1.44 V | 4.24 V |
| 50 % | 0.84 V | 1.35 A | -1.32 to -1.12 V | 3.50 V |
| 75 % | 1.22 V | 2.06 A | -0.96 to -0.75 V | 2.70 V |
| 100 % | 1.65 V | 2.86 A | -0.53 to -0.32 V | 1.78 V |

Set points: V_REF of 0.38 V for 0.5 A, 0.65 V for 1 A, 1.18 V for 2 A. Measure
at U201 pin 3 or 5.

Worked example (50 %, F = 4.86 V, V_N = -12 V):

    V_T   = -12 * (5 + 3.3) / (27 + 5 + 3.3)        = -2.822 V
    R_T   = 5 + 27 ∥ 8.3                             = 11.35 kΩ
    V_REF = 4.86 + (-2.822 - 4.86) * 100 / 186.35   = 0.738 V
    I_trip = (0.738 - 0.109) / 0.538                 = 1.17 A

A Qucs-S/ngspice model of the same network gave V_REF = 0.7375 V not tripped
and -1.4208 V latched (equations: 0.7379 V and -1.4214 V).

Tolerance and drift:

- Unit spread (1 % resistors, RV1 ±10 %, +5 V ±3 %, negative rail -11.2 to
  -12.0 V): low stop between zero and 0.56 A; high stop between 2.6 and 3.1 A.
- 100 mV on the negative rail moves the trip about 0.03 A; 50 mV on +5 V about
  0.04 A.
- Below about V_REF = 0.15 V the comparator trips at zero current and cannot
  be reset.
- Guidance: about 0.5 A for bring-up and the 5 W build; 0.9 A or more for
  10 W, where each FET averages about 0.6 A.

### 4.6 Latch and clamp

The latch holds when the latched V_REF is below the smallest value of V_m,
about 0.1 V after comparator offset. The table in 4.5 shows it is at least
0.4 V below that at every setting.

D9 (1N5711, anode to GND, cathode to V_REF) conducts about 35 µA and holds
V_REF near -0.2 to -0.25 V, inside the LM393's -0.3 V input limit.

When not tripped the diode is reverse-biased. The node's source resistance is

    R_node = R16 ∥ (R17 + R_T)  ≈ 45 kΩ

so reverse leakage shifts V_REF by about 45 mV per microamp (about 0.08 A of
trip current per microamp). A low-leakage small-signal Schottky is required; a
power Schottky such as the 1N5817 is not suitable.

### 4.7 What a trip removes

| Path | Mechanism | Time constant | End state |
|---|---|---|---|
| PA gate bias | U202 switches 1, 2 open; R219, R212 (2.2 kΩ) discharge about 200 nF on the PA board | 0.44 ms | 0 V |
| DRIVER_BIAS | MCP4922 reference follows FAULT_N through R47 and C48 | 0.02 ms | Programmed value x FAULT_N / 4.96; at most 0.45 V for a 3 V setting |
| Envelope | Same reference | 0.02 ms | Gate 2 at -3.2 V or lower |

Envelope stage, with R_in = R36 + R34 ∥ R35 = 10.99 kΩ, V_ref = 0.99 x V_Z and
R22 = 10 kΩ:

    V_G2 = (1 + R22 / R_in) * V_DAC - (R22 / R_in) * V_ref
         = 1.91 * V_DAC - 4.59                [V_Z = 5.1 V]

With a realistic Zener voltage of 4.8 V at 7 mA the offset is -4.32 V. During a
fault the DAC output cannot exceed the FAULT_N low level (0.75 V worst case),
so gate 2 is at most 1.91 * 0.75 - 4.59 = -3.16 V.

### 4.8 Response and reset timing

- Sense filter: 5 kΩ with 100 nF, 0.5 ms.
- Bias discharge: 0.44 ms.
- Over-current to PA cut-off: about 1 ms, inside the IRF510's safe operating
  area at 13.8 V.

During the 1 ms reset pulse, R46 (2.2 kΩ) charges C14 and, through R47, C48
(22 nF). FAULT_N reaches 3.15 V (bias switches close) in 0.06 ms, 4.48 V
(releases a 0.5 A setting) in 0.17 ms, and 4.80 V in 0.25 ms. PA bias and
drive return for the length of the pulse.

## 5. Power, heartbeat and start-up

### 5.1 Rails

|---|---|---|---|

| +12 V | U8 LM2940CT-12 | Metro (via F1, J21), U1, U11, Q2, D3, U7 | About 110 mA. Needs about 12.8 V at J5 to regulate |
| -12 V | U7 LT1054 from +12 V | U1, U11, R59 | About 11 mA; sits near -11.6 V |
| +5 V | U6 LM2940CT-5.0, fed through R44 (15 Ω 5 W) | Logic, DACs, breakouts, LCD, encoder, PA sense amp | About 300 mA. U6 peaks at 1.2 W; fit a heatsink |
| +3.3 V | U13 MCP1700 from +5 V | Pull-ups, U2 low side, U14 | Separate from the Metro's own 3.3 V |
| SS_PWR_PRE | Input rail through K6 | R42, K5, LPF relay coils (J2 pin 8), J34 indicator via K5's NC contact and R7 | Live when K6 is closed |
| SS_PA_Power | SS_PWR_PRE through R42 (24 Ω 10 W), or directly through K5 | PA board via J33 | Pre-charges C6 with a 0.11 s time constant |

### 5.2 Heartbeat and relay drive

- **Watchdog.** U18A is a retriggerable one-shot of 0.47 s (R30 x C41), fired
  by each high-to-low edge on U17 P12. Its Q output drives Q7; its inverted
  output drives Q10, which lights the indicator on J32 when the heartbeat is
  absent.
- **Relay drive.** K6's coil returns through R43 and Q6 (U17 P13); K5's through
  R15 and Q5 (U17 P14). Both Q5 and Q6 return through Q7, so neither relay can
  operate without the heartbeat. Coils are fed from the input rail through
  90 Ω; D5 and D7 are the flyback diodes.
- **Relay roles.** K6 is the main feed: it connects the input rail to
  SS_PWR_PRE, which reaches the PA through R42. K5 shorts R42. K5 alone cannot
  power the PA, and K6 stays closed during operation.
- **Power-on reset.** C63 and C64 (1 µF) with R48 and R62 (10 kΩ) hold both
  halves of U18 in reset for about 10 ms after +5 V appears.
- **Fail-safe.** If the heartbeat stops, Q7 turns off, K5 and K6 open, and the
  PA and the LPF relays lose power. C6 keeps SS_PWR_PRE up for a second or two
  through R42.
- **J34 indicator.** Lit while K6 is closed and K5 is not, i.e. during
  pre-charge.

### 5.3 Start-up sequence

1. Write U17 with P13, P14, P15 and P2 to P5 low. Until this write, all of
   them are high.
2. Write both MCP4922 channels and MCP4728 channels A and B to zero.
3. Start the heartbeat on P12.
4. Pulse FAULT_CLEAR and confirm FAULT_N reads high.
5. Set P13 high to close K6. The PA rail pre-charges through R42 and the LPF
   relays gain power.
6. Select a band relay. Toggle T/R and confirm TRANSMIT_N goes low and returns
   high; this checks the relay, the cable and the coil supply.
7. After at least 0.5 s, set P14 high to close K5.
8. Apply PA bias, then driver bias, then key with the envelope DAC only after
   TRANSMIT_N reads low.

With K6 closed and K5 open, R42 limits PA current to 0.56 A and dissipates at
most 7.5 W into a dead short, so this state is safe for bring-up.

## 7. Firmware obligations

- Follow the start-up sequence in 5.3, in particular K6 before K5 with a 0.5 s
  wait.
- Keep the heartbeat toggling on U17 P12 with a falling edge at least every
  0.47 s; twice that rate gives margin.
- Select the MCP4922 buffered-reference mode. Channel A is DRIVER_BIAS and
  channel B is the envelope.
- Hold BS170 average current below about 35 mA each. Nothing in hardware
  limits DRIVER_BIAS.
- Scale the envelope per band for the 7 dB drive slope, and store each 40673's
  turn-on threshold (near 1.3 V at the DAC).
- Servo PA idle current from the per-FET readings (amps = volts / 0.55).
- On FAULT_N low: write zero to both MCP4922 channels and both PA bias
  channels, then pulse FAULT_CLEAR, then re-check FAULT_N.
- Key only after TRANSMIT_N reads low, and never switch a band or T/R relay
  with RF present.
- Program the MCP4728 to address 0x67 with SW1 isolating the Si5351, and store
  zero in its EEPROM for channels A and B.
- Configure the LEDC peripheral on A1 for the sidetone square wave at the user's
  selected pitch (500 to 1000 Hz, default 700 Hz). Set once at boot and leave it
  free-running; do not try to gate the LEDC on and off per dit. GATE_2 handles
  keying for both the RF path and the sidetone.
