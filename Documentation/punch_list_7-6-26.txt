HIGH PRIORITY -- decide before ordering PCB
============================================

[ ] H1. C2 (MAINS_HEARTBEAT / K_MAIN interlock)
        The CD14538B on Q5 protects only the ON-BOARD HV contactor driver.
        The MAINS-SIDE K_MAIN contactor (upstream of the HV supply, in the
        supply project) is not yet handed off to the analog board.

        Decide between:
        [ ] (a) Use CD14538B section B on the arduino sheet to generate a
                second hardened heartbeat that exits via a dedicated
                connector (or spare umbilical pin) to the supply project's
                K_MAIN driver. Same monostable-hardened topology on both
                HV switches.
        [ ] (b) Route a raw GPIO heartbeat via a dedicated connector to
                the supply project, and let the supply project's own
                monostable do the enforcement.

        Once decided, add the connector footprint and a global label
        on the arduino sheet.

[ ] H2. C3 (+12V rail source)
        Three coils tie to +12V (J6 T/R, J7 HV contactor, J8 HV in-rush
        bypass). No +12V source is drawn anywhere on the board.

        Decide between:
        [ ] (a) Metro Vin passthrough (puts Metro on the safety-critical
                path -- USB disconnect kills +5V too)
        [ ] (b) Dedicated +12V input connector on the analog board
                (RECOMMENDED -- cheap to add now, expensive after fab)
        [ ] (c) Local regulation from a higher rail

        If (b), add a 2-pin connector footprint to the arduino sheet with
        a +12V power symbol on pin 1 and GND on pin 2, plus a bulk cap
        (10uF or larger) and 100nF bypass right at the connector.

[ ] H3. U18 (CD14538B) VDD rail choice
        Datasheet V_IH_min at VDD=5V is 3.5V. The heartbeat trigger comes
        from PCF8575 U17 pin P12. What rail is U17 powered from?

        [ ] Confirm PCF8575 U17 supply rail (should be either +5V or +3.3V).
        [ ] If U17 is on +5V, U18 at +5V is fine -- no change needed.
        [ ] If U17 is on +3.3V, move U18 to +3.3V:
                - VDD tie: change #PWR0120 from +5V to +3.3V
                - VDD tie for reset A/B and unused-section ties: also to +3.3V
                - Q7 gate drive still fine (2N7000 Vgs(th) ~2V)

        At VDD=3V the V_IH_min drops to ~2.1V, comfortably below any
        3.3V logic HIGH.
